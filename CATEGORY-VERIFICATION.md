# Reviewed category release

## Why the pool had only 20 people

The server catalog contains 291 people. The production gate requires an explicit editorial Tea review, sourced public-life coverage, an iconic work/milestone, an interesting non-basic story, and reviewed musical-group/collaboration history when relevant. Only 20 profiles had those reviews. A category with no eligible profiles was disabled as “coming soon”; the missing decks were a data-coverage issue, not a CSS or cache issue.

This release adds 20 reviews without weakening the gate. The pool has 40 people and 161 distinct safe Tea cards. Bollywood, Cricket, Football and Writers each have five people. India has nine overlapping profiles. The other 251 catalog entries remain excluded until reviewed.

Sources include public statements/interviews, Yale, Nobel Prize, author estates/foundations, ICC, UEFA, AP and established news publications. See `lib/game/category-profiles.ts` for the individual receipts. Empty fields mean no reviewed record, never proof that no relationship, rumor or controversy existed. Musical groups are distinct from sports teams and acting companies. The existing reviewed Music profiles retain their band histories.
